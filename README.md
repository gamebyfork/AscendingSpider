**Ascending Spider** is a twist on the classic Spider Solitaire. Instead of building sequences from King down to Ace, your goal is to build them _ascending: from Ace to King._
### 3 Game Modes:
- Standard: Classic solitaire with no time limits.
- Blitz: You have exactly 3 minutes to collect all suits.
- Endless: Collect suits, receive new decks, and set records for the most loops completed.

**Achievements & Unlockables:** win games, earn achievements and unlock exclusive card back designs.

_Translated in English, Russian, Spanish, Chinese and Japanese._

### Controls:
- Left Mouse Button — Drag and drop cards. Short click — auto-move.
- Z — Undo move.
- H — Hint.
- F — Toggle Fullscreen.
- Esc — Pause / Menu.
